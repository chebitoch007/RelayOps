# PRODUCT.md

Product truth for RelayOps (source: docs/00_PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md, docs/product/PRODUCT.md, docs/website/*).

## What it is
RelayOps is a one-person AI automation studio (founder: Brian Chebitoch, Kenya) that builds lead follow-up,
chatbot, WhatsApp, booking and workflow systems for marketing agencies and service businesses.
It is a services business, not software.

## Stage (be honest on the page)
Pre-revenue. No paying-client case studies or testimonials yet. One documented reference build
(Lead Qualification & Booking) exists. Never invent metrics, logos, quotes or client results.
Illustrative content must be labelled as an example.

## Visitor
A skeptical agency owner or service-business owner, usually arriving from a cold email on a phone.
Question in their head: "Is this real, and is it worth a 30-minute call?"

## Page job (Persuade mode)
Convert to, in order: (1) book a discovery call (Calendly), (2) send the contact form, (3) email Brian.
Organic search now also matters: pages must be fully server-rendered and crawlable.

## Voice
Plain, direct, engineer-honest. Outcome-first ("a lead gets a reply in seconds"), no hype, no "revolutionary".
No em dashes. Say what the system will not do as readily as what it does.

## Anti-references
Dark navy + neon glass-card "AI agency" template, gradient text, fake dashboards, skeleton loaders,
particle backgrounds, icon-plus-heading card grids.
